=== what was proved per cell, and not assumed ===
  A cell is only measured if its configuration can be read back from the running
  shape. Three arms are new here and each is a different claim, so each is checked
  with `volucer check` on the file the live server was started from:

    volucer-unix     transport reported as `socket on unix:<prefix>.<slot>.sock`
    volucer-attach   `relay` is the harness's socket, and `connections` == workers
    volucer-prefork  `children = N per slot`, and the PHP process total is
                     workers * N + workers

  opcache is proved the same way the committed 8.5 matrix proves it: the wrapper's
  own interpreter reports `active=1` before any cell runs.

=== two passes, and why ===
  The first pass measured volucer-worker, -unix, -attach and -fastcgi completely:
  90 cells each, 24 memory samples each, zero failures. It lost volucer-prefork
  entirely to a harness bug — the `children` sweep was echoed comma-joined, so
  `children = 1,4,8,16,24,32` reached the TOML as one integer and every one of
  those cells failed to parse before it started:

    SKIP volucer-prefork w=1  on: not ready      <- this pass, and it was the harness
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=4  on: not ready
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=8  on: not ready
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=16 on: not ready
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=24 on: not ready
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=32 on: not ready

  Those six lines are kept above rather than deleted because a matrix whose
  skipped cells are invisible is a matrix that can be read as complete. Each arm
  is started, measured and stopped on its own, so the prefork pass is the same
  measurement the first pass would have produced had the bug not been there; the
  two TSVs are concatenated on the `arm` column.

=== cells the host could not hold ===
  `children` multiplies the process count, and this host has 3.8 GB. Cells whose
  PHP process total exceeded MAX_PHP_PROCESSES=96 were skipped rather than started,
  because a run that swaps measures the swap:

    SKIP volucer-prefork w=4  on c=24: 100 processes over the cap
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=4  on c=32: 132 processes over the cap
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=8  on c=16: 136 processes over the cap
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=8  on c=24: 200 processes over the cap
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=8  on c=32: 264 processes over the cap
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=16 on c=8:  144 processes over the cap

    SKIP volucer-prefork w=16 on c=32: 528 processes over the cap
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=24 on c=4:  120 processes over the cap

    SKIP volucer-prefork w=24 on c=24: 600 processes over the cap
    SKIP volucer-prefork w=24 on c=32: 792 processes over the cap

  separate the two things `children` trades — more processes per socket versus
  more sockets — because both ladders are present at the same total.
